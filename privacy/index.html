Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how the PhotoFit iPhone app handles your information. In short: your photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. The free version shows optional ads from Google, which collects some data as described below.

Your photos

  • Resizing, compression and format conversion happen entirely on your iPhone.
  • PhotoFit works on a copy. Your original photo is never changed or deleted. Working copies are kept in the app's private storage, excluded from backups, and deleted when you clear them in Settings or automatically after 24 hours.
  • By default PhotoFit removes metadata, including location, from the photos it creates. You can change this in the app.
  • PhotoFit asks only for permission to add photos to your library when you save a result. It can't read your library; you choose photos with the system picker.
  • When you share a result, it goes only to the app or person you choose.

What we collect

We don't operate accounts, servers or analytics for PhotoFit, and we don't receive your photos. Your settings and saved presets are stored only on your device.

Purchases

PhotoFit Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads that unlock batches. Where the law requires consent, Google's consent form appears the first time you tap Watch an Ad, and PhotoFit doesn't start Google's ad SDK before you answer it. Once ads are allowed (you've answered the form, or consent isn't required where you are), PhotoFit starts Google's ad SDK when the app opens and loads an ad in advance, so it's ready if you choose to watch one. You can change your choices at any time in Settings, under Privacy Choices. Google may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps.

PhotoFit asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before the advertising identifier can be used. If you decline, ads still work without it and nothing else changes. With PhotoFit Pro, PhotoFit doesn't start the ad SDK at launch and never loads or shows ads. If you buy Pro during a session, any loaded ad is discarded right away.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we delete them on request.

Children

PhotoFit isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if our practices change. Questions: support@photofit.live.
